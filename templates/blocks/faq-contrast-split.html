Contrast Split FAQ

A dark split composition gives support content stronger presence without sacrificing reading order or interaction contrast.

Before you ship

Know exactly what the system guarantees

Short answers make the technical contract visible to product and engineering teams.

Are focus states included?
Yes. Interactive elements preserve a visible focus-visible treatment across supported themes.
Does motion respect user preferences?
Yes. Semantic durations collapse when reduced motion is requested while state changes remain understandable.
Can a theme break component APIs?
No. Themes remap semantic values; selectors, attributes, states, and markup contracts remain stable.
Is Shadow DOM used?
No. All custom elements keep their structure in Light DOM so the cascade remains inspectable and adaptable.

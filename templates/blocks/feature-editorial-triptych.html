Editorial Triptych Feature

A three-column editorial composition balances two real photographs with one high-contrast product statement.

Structure the foundation, then leave room for character

Three equal columns create a deliberate rhythm without forcing image, message, and supporting copy into the same treatment.

Foundation

Make every decision traceable

Named tokens and contracts connect intent to implementation.

Principle
Consistency should make the product clearer, not make every page identical.

Use shared rules to protect meaning while composition stays expressive.

Expression

Let content shape the composition

Responsive grids preserve hierarchy while adapting to each story.

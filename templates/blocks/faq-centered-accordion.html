Centered Accordion FAQ

A balanced introduction and single-open disclosure list keep common product questions easy to scan.

Questions, answered clearly

Start with the essentials, then open only the detail you need.

What is included in Ren10?
Tokens, responsive layouts, accessible components, patterns, examples, and agent-readable contracts ship together.
Do I need a framework?
No. Ren10 is built for vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with progressive custom elements where behavior is useful.
Does it support dark mode?
Yes. Semantic color tokens adapt through color-scheme and can also be controlled with a theme attribute.
Can components be themed?
Yes. Override documented semantic or component tokens without rewriting internal selectors.

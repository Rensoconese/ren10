Adoption Sequence Feature

A compact photographic introduction and four numbered stages explain a practical adoption path in one balanced section.

From first audit to shared practice

Introduce the system without stopping the product

Adoption works best as a visible sequence of useful improvements rather than a single disruptive migration.

  1. Map repeated decisions

    Find the patterns teams already rebuild, debate, and maintain in parallel.

  2. Ground the foundation

    Name tokens, semantics, and accessibility expectations before extracting parts.

  3. Ship one useful path

    Prove the contract in a real workflow with documentation and tests attached.

  4. Expand through evidence

    Use adoption signals and product outcomes to decide what the system absorbs next.

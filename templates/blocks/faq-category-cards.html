Using Ren10

Product foundations

Which tokens should consumers use?
Use semantic color, spacing, type, and motion tokens, plus documented component overrides.
Can layouts be nested?
Yes, but keep primitives shallow and flatten wrappers when one grid can express the relationship.

Shipping Ren10

Delivery and quality

What is the accessibility baseline?
WCAG 2.1 AA is the baseline, including visible focus, labeled controls, and keyboard access.
How should blocks be tested?
Verify responsive layout, keyboard behavior, light and dark themes, reduced motion, and browser rendering.

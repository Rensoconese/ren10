FAQ 9 · Accent panel

Accent Panel FAQ

A strong orientation panel and neutral disclosure column create hierarchy without sacrificing answer contrast or scanability.

Before you begin

Make adoption a product decision

Start with one real flow, keep its semantics intact, and expand only after the team has evidence.

A useful first milestone

Ship one responsive, keyboard-complete page using only documented tokens and components.

Should we migrate everything at once?
No. Start with a representative flow, verify the working model, and migrate adjacent surfaces in deliberate increments.
How should legacy styles be handled?
Map repeated intent to semantic tokens, replace layout hacks with primitives, and remove old selectors only after visual and behavior checks pass.
Who owns adoption quality?
Design, engineering, and product share the outcome. The system provides contracts; the delivery team validates the real experience.
What should block a release?
Broken keyboard paths, unreadable contrast, overflow, undocumented overrides, or a mismatch between component contracts and shipped behavior.
